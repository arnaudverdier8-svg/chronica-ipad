## Critique response (v1 -> v2): every valid high-impact fix applied; rejections explained
**Light / relief (all three critics, P0/P1) - applied, one part rejected.**  Key:fill 3.6 -> 4.6 (bible allows 1:2.5-1:6; the storyboard's Act I "3:1" is not the library's `fill_ratio`, whose fill_i = key_i / ratio x 0.35); the window pool is now steep (peak on the capital, 8 % floor, aspect 2.0, diagonal window gradient 35 %: corners 0.15-0.3 of the peak, exposure 0.9) plus one lift along the thread's first tie-down; AO halo (v1: 40 % darkening up to 1.5 mm from every motif = the 'sticker shadow') replaced by `ao_v2` (k1 .95, k2 .22, floor .5); **real structural relief baked as padded underlay** (ridges .20 mm, towns .45-.70 mm, capital .8-1.15 mm, capital rise .95 mm, bridge .5 mm; couching bars lifted to sit on the padding) so hills, towns and the capital cast their own height-field shadows; visible cloth: creases through the empty sky and the border (1.25 / .7 mm), vertical valley fold with dust, blotchy storage tone, split-tone grade (cool brown-violet shadows, warm highlights).  Measured breath (3.3 px/mm, `breath/breath_stats.json`): shadow tip of the p99-height feature (1.65 mm) moves 7 px for az 120->150, 5.5 px for el 22->16, 9.9 px for the combined swing; the tallest features (2.9 mm incl. fold) up to ~17 px; 17 % / 31 % / 55 % of the pixels change by more than 8/255.  **Rejected**: "hills 2-3 mm, walls 2-4 mm": wool p95 would be 2-3x p1's (kit is already +44 % at 1.31 mm vs p1 .91 mm, which breaks the 'identical craft' of G1) and contradicts flat laid-and-couched Bayeux work.  "Raise key:fill contrast to 1.8-2" (critic 3) is the opposite of critic 1; I went the way the bible allows (4.6).
**Sticker / appliqué tell - applied.**  AO fix above; contours are now stitched in 7-20 mm runs with their own width (+/-18 %), tone, stitch length and rope height, overlapping, ink = 66 % dark ink + 34 % local fill hue (no uniform halo stroke); wool heights ride on padding with fuzz overlap; the crown is moored (two gold tethers with crimson ties).  The kit has no blurred drop shadow: the 'drop shadow' was the height-field shadow of a uniformly 1 mm plateau, now varied by padding.
**Flat wool fills - applied.**  Per-strand dye jitter (L sigma 5 %, +/-warm/cool tilt) on top of the library's lots, dye-lot patches +/-5 % over 28 mm on the big fields, masonry courses (3.2 mm bands in alternating stones, 15 % flips) with vertical joints instead of vertical-bar 'picket' walls, striped / tiled roofs (darker second dye every 1.7-2 mm), per-run outline tones.  Not done (library, read-only): per-strand anisotropic sheen / twist normals on wool.
**Palette and age - applied.**  Palette re-specified in OKLCH: wool median L **.458 vs p1 .443** (v1 .553), chroma p50 .055 / p95 .088 (p1 .067 / .112), the four house dyes in one chroma band (.085-.13, woad C .085, crimson C .13); linen mottle + dust + extra foxing (clustered at the hem, bible 4.7) + two tide stains; ageing base .26 + hem .5.  Not claimed: the frame is still Act I 'near fresh' by design (palette.json); HSV saturation p95 is unchanged (.745) because dark muted wool has a high HSV saturation, the OKLCH chroma is what fell.
**Outlines - applied** (above).  **Linen weave at Nyquist - partly:** shading is now done on the 7 px/mm level and prefiltered into 5 px/mm (v1 shaded the 5 px/mm mip raw) and the weave LOD ramp starts at 1.8 px; at 400 % the weave is a regular basket grid (hp std .018 vs v1 .011); `look.lod_p0 = 2.4` (`shot_f91_lod.json`) gives .013 at the cost of 9 % wool HF: use it for the moving frames if the shimmer gate (G7) asks.  The periphery is not 'featureless' any more (hp .037 / .011).
**Gold thread - applied.**  Linear gold (0.80, 0.46, 0.115) -> OKLCH L .75 C .126 h 81; ply 1.5 mm (7 px twist readable at 5 px/mm), k_env .8 for the glints, accent light along the thread, slack wander +/-3 mm (a ruler in v1); the tie-down is a 3-turn silk lashing, 5.8 mm across the pair with a 6 mm loose tail, ~3x the area of v1's tick.  **Not done:** thread 'lifted off the line before the tie' (the library's height per stitch is constant along a path).
**Bridge and river - applied.**  Arch bridge in front of the water, directly under the road, two-colour voussoir ring (33 mm outer diameter) + keystone, masonry piers (46 mm wide body), road dye changes at both ends; river 13 mm wide opening to ~35 mm (v1 5 -> 21).
**Towns / clip-art - partly.**  Each town has its own roof dye set, wall tones, tower tones and masonry; the capital is the only woad-coned keep and the tallest; the large town's tower was lowered (vstretch 1.55 -> 1.45) and its pennant pole shortened.  **Not done:** new building language (arcades, domes): the storyboard fixes the towns as stitched settle_* elevations and the game models' round towers with conical roofs remain the silhouette; the hamlet is hand-drawn (settle_0's palisade reads as hay).
**Focal hierarchy / composition / landscape - applied.**  Capital 96 mm walls (vs 92) with a taller keep, brightest pool on it, padded rise; interiors of the back ridge, capital rise and middle ridge are now stitched (laid fields / ploughed strips) so the empty ovals are gone (linen 47.9 %); the far ridge fills the sky above the hamlet; the lions are 1.25x; the border rhythm is broken (bars at 14 irregular positions, 3 flower kinds, varied heights).
**4:3 safe zone - applied.**  Tie-down at frame x 450 (>= 320), the hamlet's left cottage starts at ~295 px (25 px = 5 mm outside the 4:3 zone; the tower, the other cottages and everything else of the hamlet are inside), the large town ends at ~2240 px, the other towns and the capital are inside x 320-2240.

**Delivery - applied.**  The 8-bit copy is graded from float with the grade's grain before quantisation (v1: a plain >> 8 of the 16-bit master).  The 16-bit master has 5156 distinct levels in a 60x200 px smooth-linen patch (the 8-bit copy 49: the patch spans ~50 display levels).
